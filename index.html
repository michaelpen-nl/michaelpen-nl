01 / About

I am born and raised in the town of Den Helder, the Netherlands, where I completed pre-university education before moving to Groningen for my studies. I completed the BSc. Economics and Business Economics in 2021, after which I started a pre-MSc programme for Econometrics, Operations Research and Actuarial Studies. In 2023, I graduated cum laude for the MSc. Economics and MSc Econometrics, Operations Research and Actuarial Studies. After my studies, I worked as a Data Analyst at an entrepreneurship that provided solutions to topics in mobility. Since September 2024, I am a PhD candidate in the field of econometrics at the University of Groningen, Faculty of Economics and Business. During my PhD, I will focus on developing methods for and applications with random coefficients models.

02 / Expertise

I am a PhD candidate in econometrics at the University of Groningen’s Faculty of Economics and Business. My main research interests are random coefficients models, (semi-)nonparametric estimation, and applications in health and labour economics.
